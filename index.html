ABOUT / WHO WE ARE

A logistics partner you can count on

Blue Dolphin Enterprise (BDE) is an Oman-based logistics and freight forwarding company built on a foundation of reliability, precision, and genuine client care. Operating from Muscat, we specialise in moving goods seamlessly across borders while navigating the complexities of customs, compliance, and last-mile delivery throughout Oman and the GCC.

From single consignments to complex multi-modal freight, BDE offers the full range of logistics services that modern businesses need — underpinned by a commitment to transparency and on-time performance.
